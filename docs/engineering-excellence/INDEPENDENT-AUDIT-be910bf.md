# Independent audit of PostRust engineering excellence

**Withhold acceptance at `be910bf`.** A classification retry can make a valid memory-lane journal impossible to reopen. A save/restore round trip can change which event an unchanged public scheduler call suspends. The 5k and 20k entity measurements also overstate the population doing recurring work. Each affects a claim the charter treats as material. [Acceptance criteria](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/docs/engineering-excellence/DIRECTIVE.md#L279-L281)

**Scope.** Baseline `pre-excellence-5b93167` peels to `5b9316742b2f9fe4d0fca2a9743275f91a2ff6aa`. The audited branch and remote both resolved to `be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c`. I inspected the program diff and its history, parsed the archived Windows results, compared selected OpenCode source messages, and ran targeted Rust and GPU witnesses on disposable fixtures. The GPU was an RTX 5070 Ti, driver 617.42. Full batteries and Linux execution were outside this audit. Source links refer to the audited head unless labeled otherwise.

## 1. Self-argued invariants

### IA-01 · blocker · Retrying a classification sync can break cold recovery

**Reproduced on the public entry point.** Start with the encounter and late reputation classification from the B04 test. Confirm record episode 0. For the classification flush, open the existing journal with its `AfterSync / InjectError` seam. Move the disposable journal temporarily aside while its write handle stays open, so the sync succeeds and reconciliation cannot reread the path. Restore the pathname before the next submit. This separates a successful durable write from a transient verification failure. **Remaining error paths.** The constant unit span and fixed envelope are valid in this construction. The current plan encoder always returns `Ok`. Initial stage failure records an unjournaled identity. Definite capture rejection discards the staged envelope and records that identity. Initial apply failure after a successful capture records both the durable snapshot and a journaled identity. Success records the same snapshot. On completion, however, the caller first `take()`s the marker. Restage errors, capture errors other than definite rejection, and the final `mark_applied()?` can all return without restoring the marker. Only a second definite rejection explicitly re-arms it. Under an unchanged, consistent pipeline, immediate apply after successful capture ordinarily succeeds. The two B04 tests construct their unresolved state by hand, and the journaled test even inserts the snapshot itself. They cannot establish that production failure handling makes those writes. ### IA-02 · major · A stateful scheduler predicate observes heap layout after restore

Schedule one near-lane event at each due time `[1, 3, 2]` in that insertion order. Pass `suspend_matching` a closure that increments a counter and accepts its second call. Compiled against pre-lane `e530cea`, it visits `[1, 2, 3]` and holds due 2. At the audited head it visits `[1, 3, 2]` and holds due 3. More decisively, at **the same head**, save before suspension and restore a second spine from those bytes. The two spines initially serialize identically. The identical closure holds due 3 on the original and due 2 on the restored spine. The resulting saves differ. Reclassification uses the same traversal for the source lane. [Pre-lane pop loop](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/e530cea347d207921f696d19d562c8cab6175b85/crates/engine-core/src/spine.rs#L1135-L1147) [spine.rs:930-943](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/spine.rs#L930-L943) [spine.rs:1193-1204](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/spine.rs#L1193-L1204) [spine.rs:1273-1284](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/spine.rs#L1273-L1284) [spine.rs:2482-2495](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/spine.rs#L2482-L2495)

The narrow heap argument holds. `Reverse<Pending>` orders by `(due, seq)`, scheduling allocates fresh sequences, and restore rejects duplicate unfinished identities. Both extraction iterators are exhausted. The map passes retain sequence order. The missing premise is that **membership** must be independent of visitation order. The public `FnMut` API has no such restriction. With the pure predicate `due == 2`, my two builds produced byte-identical saves, and each decoded and re-encoded the other build's bytes exactly (SHA-256 `1c27bc95c42381d40ee5e3d8a391ca74b1c4eafa064458b76a851e5cacba5ef7`). The program's larger old/new probe transcripts also match, for their class-based predicates. [spine.rs:317-350](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/spine.rs#L317-L350) [spine.rs:771-779](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/spine.rs#L771-L779) [spine.rs:2281-2297](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/spine.rs#L2281-L2297) [spine.rs:3085-3140](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/spine.rs#L3085-L3140) [FINDINGS.md:226-233](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/docs/engineering-excellence/FINDINGS.md#L226-L233)

### IA-03 · major · Five finite ticks put infinity in a live particle

Use a zero point volume, rate 34, velocity `[f32::MAX, 0, 0]`, zero jitter and gravity, life `[1,1]`, drag 1, no kill plane, seed 1. Construction succeeds. Call `tick(0.03_f32)` four times, then `tick(f32::from_bits(0x3f6147af))`. The first particle is still in the public active slice with **X = +∞ and life = 5.9604645e-8**. The final tick is smaller than its stored remaining life. Position addition and life subtraction round separately. The proof uses exact subtraction to infer a bound on elapsed time, which the implementation cannot guarantee. Active positions enter the fingerprint. [particle.rs:291-328](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/particle.rs#L291-L328) [particle.rs:498-526](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/particle.rs#L498-L526) [particle.rs:555-560](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/particle.rs#L555-L560) [B05-particle-domain-contract.md:206-246](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/docs/engineering-excellence/designs/B05-particle-domain-contract.md#L206-L246)

The two committed refusal tests do use representable inputs. In the combined-Y test, each term is finite and the sum overflows. The velocity witness isolates its gate because `2e38 + 3e38 × 0.5` overflows while its displacement bound is finite. Its **positive** initial Y velocity moves toward zero under downward gravity. It tests the conservative magnitude check. A **negative** initial Y velocity gives the corresponding physical overflow direction. The invalid design literal `6e38` is unnecessary in either case. [particle.rs:2115-2163](https://github.com/Willpatpost/PostRust/blob/be910bf7fcd2b3d41e83de16ce42ad6c4caeaf5c/crates/engine-core/src/particle.rs#L2115-L2163)

**Disposition.** Reopen B05's finite-survivor guarantee. Define a deterministic refusal or checked update for actual integrated state, or prove an admission bound with adequate floating-point margin over supported step sequences. Keep existing campaign digests and add the five-tick witness.

### IA-04 · major · The kill-plane observer can receive infinity on the first tick

### IA-05 · major · A live classification submit cannot finish its own uncertain canonical commit

I repeated the late-classification fixture with the canonical medium's `AfterRootDirSync / InjectError` seam and kept the same pipeline. The first submit returned `Ok([])`. The next two returned `AttemptUnresolved` for the same revision. The bridge handles only `Confirmed`, then returns the ordinary empty success for `Unknown` or `StaleParent`. Its next call goes straight back to `commit_canonical`, which refuses while a candidate is pending. The existing ambiguity test drops the pipeline and reopens it before trying again. This is a progress failure at the slice boundary. The verified canonical copy can still be recovered.
